## 4. What is missing, by fit to ₹5–10L

1. **Insider / promoter-purchase drift** — the same shape as results drift, from a free feed (SEBI
   PIT disclosures on NSE): 90-day CAR +6.7 %, concentrated in smaller firms (Springer 2026); a
   2015–2024 NSE/BSE event study positive; promoters' purchases the largest effect but the most
   mimicked (Emerald). Slots into `research/events.py` as a second event type; one slow event book
   (results + insider buys, 20-session holds) is the first real product.
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40622-026-00473-3 · https://www.ijllr.com/post/do-insider-trades-predict-future-stock-returns-evidence-from-indian-stock-market ·
   https://www.emerald.com/ajar/article/6/2/210/60015/Effect-of-insider-trading-on-stock-characteristics
2. **The closing-auction imbalance** — new since August and already on our feed: the 15:15–15:3x
   frames carry the auction's indicative price and quantity, which the aggregator now counts as
   `auction_ticks` and discards. Record the series (price, indicative quantity per frame) and, at
   twenty sessions, test imbalance → auction price vs the 15:15 last, and → the next open. The
   restructured F&O opening auction the same way. No published Indian evidence yet — that is the point.
3. **A momentum sleeve as the beta book** — the one factor with robust Indian evidence: Nifty200
   Momentum 30 ~15–19 % CAGR, Sharpe ~0.87 vs 0.68 for Nifty, drawdowns −60 to −75 %, the premium
   concentrated in illiquid names. Monthly rebalance, 12-1 formation, no stops; it is what "1–2 % a
   month with drawdowns" looks like.
   https://www.niftyindices.com/docs/default-source/indices/nifty200-momentum-30/momentum-strategy-whitepaper_2026.pdf ·
   https://insights.dsij.in/dsijarticledetail/why-momentum-investing-matters-33675 · https://freefincal.com/is-indian-momentum-investing-just-a-liquidity-illusion/ ·
   https://faculty.iima.ac.in/iffm/Indian-Fama-French-Momentum/drawdown.php
4. **Tender-offer buybacks** — announcement effects documented (IMFI 2024); the small-shareholder
   reservation was a genuine small-capital edge, but buyback proceeds became taxable as dividend
   from Oct 2024 (background knowledge, not verified here) — check before spending time.
   https://businessperspectives.org/journals/investment-management-and-financial-innovations/issue-462/announcement-effect-of-tender-offer-share-buyback-around-turmoil-period-evidence-from-india
5. **Index rebalancing** — mixed; exclusions rebound +4–7 % over 60–240 days. A small event lane at best.
   https://ideas.repec.org/a/ids/afasfa/v5y2015i1p21-36.html · https://ideas.repec.org/a/sae/padigm/v13y2009i2p88-97.html

## 5. Infrastructure the rules make non-negotiable

- A Mumbai VPS with a static IP (regulatory now; it also ends the 30 % data loss of week 1).
- An order-rate throttle (≤ 10/s) and limit-with-protection exits in the gateway before any live route.
- Tick data instead of ~6.5 snapshots a minute for anything microstructural (the auction-imbalance
  work needs the real auction frames) — Accelpix / GFDL, already in review.

## 6. The target

3–5 % a month compounds to 40–80 % a year and needs a Sharpe above 3; the current algo's expectation
is −10 bps a trade net ≈ −2 % a month on ₹5L, and the intraday book must earn 2.6 % a month just to pay
the broker. With ₹5L, one slow event book and a Sharpe near 1, **1–2 % a month with 10–15 % drawdowns**
is the defensible ambition. The 3–5 % version exists only with leverage or short gamma.
