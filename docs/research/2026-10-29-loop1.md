## Shipped (commit below; settings in `.env`, defaults in `config.py`)
- `KN_STRATEGY_WALLS_ROUND=false` — walls are the levels on the chart: daily pivots, PDH/PDL, weekly pivots.
- `KN_STRATEGY_MAX_MOVE_ADR=0.6` — a signal after 0.6 ADR travelled from the open is recorded, not taken.
- `KN_STRATEGY_LEVELS_HL=continuous` — with `pdc=last`, the previous day's high and low from its twelve 30m bars
  before 15:15 (a gap → the daily candle). `closes` in the snapshot shows both.
- every signal carries `nearest` (name, price, bps, R): the real level inside the tolerance that the walls skip.
- Expected on the history: about 20 % fewer entries, +1 bps gross a trade, still negative net. Nothing here is an
  edge; it is the system reading its own trades correctly.
