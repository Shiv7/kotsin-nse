What the numbers can and cannot say

Six sessions of an 8 % index rally are mostly beta. The build-up split and the relative strength are the only columns that separate a stock from the index. Nothing here is a tested edge: the controlled fact base found that levels carry no information at the intraday horizon, and no multi-day stock rule has been pre-registered yet. The next step, if a list is chosen, is to write the entry, stop and holding rule down before the first trade and to score it against a random pick from the same screen.
