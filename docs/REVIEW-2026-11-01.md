# End-to-end review, 2026-11-01 — every formula, every price-action rule, every fill

Branch `fresh-base`, from `0b912b4`. Read as a quant would: every indicator and level against its
textbook definition, every backtest against the look-ahead traps in `LEARNINGS.md`, every fill
against what is orderable, every cost against the current NSE schedule, and the live engine's
data path against what the feed actually sends. Six parallel reviewers (one per module group),
every finding re-read in the code before it was acted on; the items marked **REPRO** were
reproduced with a throw-away script against the real code before being fixed.

The result: **332 tests pass** (281 before; one was failing on any machine not set to IST),
`ruff` and `import-linter` clean. No study could be re-run here (no data cache on this box); §5
lists what the owner must re-run on the laptop and why.

Everything below is either **FIXED** (in this commit, with a test), **OPEN** (a real defect, left
for a reason given) or **OWNER** (a decision the owner has to make). Numbers are the reviewers'.

## 1. Verified correct (no change)

Worth stating once, so nobody re-reviews them next month:

- **Levels** (`structure/levels.py`): floor pivots P/R1–R3/S1–S3, CPR (BC = (H+L)/2, TC = 2P−BC,
  swapped when BC > P), Camarilla H3/H4/L3/L4, weekly/monthly from the previous *complete* ISO
  week / calendar month. ADR = simple mean of H−L over the last 50 completed sessions with a split
  guard.
- **Structure** (`structure/engine.py`): FVG = bar[i−2].h < bar[i].l (filled on trade-through),
  sweep = wick through + close back, BOS on a close only, swing confirmation indexing consistent.
- **Order flow / microstructure** (`orderflow/`): L1 OFI exactly Cont–Kukanov–Stoikov 2014 in all
  six cases, multi-level OFI level by level, queue imbalance, microprice, spread in bps,
  distance-weighted imbalance, Kyle λ units, VPIN bucketing, Black-76 price / Greeks / IV (parity
  and finite differences), ATM = strike nearest the future, the complete 16-state map.
- **Bars**: bucket anchoring on the session open with 09:15 inclusive; 5m = 09:15–09:19; previous
  sessions chosen strictly by IST date everywhere; no reader touches a forming bar; the live board
  and the replay agree on which 5m bar is closed.
- **Research fills** (`confirm`, `dbcpr`, `swing`, `power`): entry at the signal bar's close,
  exits from the next bar, stop filled at min(open, stop) across a gap, same-bar stop+target →
  stop, R = net ÷ (|entry − stop| × qty); CPR/pivots from the previous session; 15m/1h reads only
  from bars whose end ≤ t; no entry on a session's last bar; no backward-window scans.
- **Events** (`events.py`): σ strictly from E−60…E−1, E = first session whose close follows the
  broadcast, the book admits by |z| after the day's exits, rolls only for expiries strictly inside
  the hold, the holdout guard. **Regimes**: the GMM refit each quarter on the past only, the
  regime at D's close applied to D+1 everywhere. **Day types**: k-means on the train months only,
  the 11:00 read from bars ≤ 10:59.
- **Costs**: STT sell-side only at the 2024-10-01 rates (equity 0.025 %, futures 0.02 %, options
  0.1 % of premium), SEBI ₹10/crore, GST 18 % on brokerage + exchange + SEBI, brokerage per order,
  option turnover on premium; no double counting between the paper fill and the runner.
